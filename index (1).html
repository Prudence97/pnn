About me

From the edit suite to AI newsrooms

I work where communication meets technology. I help people understand complex ideas, and I build the programmes and systems that turn those ideas into results.

I started with language. My first degree was in Linguistics and Communication Studies, and my undergraduate research documented endangered indigenous languages of Nigeria. From there I moved into media production. I edited long-form and short-form programmes at the Anambra Broadcasting Service, edited Nigerian comedy skits for creators like Mr Funny and Wonder D'talk, and made a short film about Rhesus factor awareness.

As misinformation became one of the biggest problems facing African media, my work moved toward AI and verification. At MyAIFactChecker I coordinated operations for an AI fact-checking tool used by more than 15,000 people. At FactCheckAfrica I led a fellowship that trained over 75 journalists to use AI tools responsibly.

I also work in growth and community. I've built pipelines and automated follow-ups for advertiser acquisition, grown an academic authors community, run digital campaigns for global advocacy and designed public health campaigns for communities in Delta State.

My PhD research at the University of Port Harcourt looks at generative AI and Nigerian Pidgin journalism, especially translation ethics and low-resource languages. It brings me back to where I started, with language.

I also write for students and young readers: a novella, a children's story about climate action, books on journalism and public relations, and a video course for new journalists.
